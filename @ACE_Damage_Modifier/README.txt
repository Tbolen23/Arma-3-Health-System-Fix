ACE Damage Modifier for Arma 3
================================

This mod modifies ACE Medical damage behavior to create a more lethal and balanced combat environment with separate systems for AI and players.

FEATURES:
---------
AI UNITS:
* 1 hit to head = instant death
* 3 hits to chest/limbs = death
* More lethal for faster AI combat

PLAYERS:
* 2 hits to head = death
* 6 hits to chest/legs = death
* Healing resets damage counters (medic gameplay viable!)
* More forgiving for extended firefights

EXPLOSIONS (SAME FOR ALL):
* Small explosions (grenades): ~5m lethal range
* Medium explosions (RPG): ~10m lethal range
* Large explosions (bombs): ~20m lethal range

REQUIREMENTS:
-------------
* Arma 3
* ACE Medical (ace_medical)
* ACE Medical Damage (ace_medical_damage)
* CBA_A3 (Community Base Addons)

INSTALLATION:
-------------
Subscribe to this mod on Steam Workshop and it will automatically download and install.
Make sure ACE and CBA mods are loaded before this mod in your mod load order.

HOW IT WORKS:
-------------
The mod integrates with ACE Medical's cardiac arrest system to apply different damage thresholds for AI vs players.
- All damage tracking is automatic and works silently
- Players can be healed to reset their damage counters
- AI gets more lethal damage for faster combat resolution
- Explosion damage applies the same way to everyone

KEY FEATURE - HEALING:
----------------------
When a player receives ACE Medical treatment, their damage counters reset!
This means medics are crucial for survival and team gameplay is encouraged.

COMPATIBILITY:
--------------
* Works with ACE Medical
* Works with ACE Advanced Medical
* Compatible with most weapon mods
* Works in singleplayer and multiplayer
* May conflict with other damage modification mods

For full documentation, visit the GitHub repository or mod workshop page.
