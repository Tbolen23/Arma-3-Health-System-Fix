[h1]ACE Damage Modifier[/h1]

A lethal combat enhancement mod that works seamlessly with ACE Medical to create a more realistic and deadly battlefield experience.

[h2]Key Features[/h2]

[b]🎯 Headshots Matter[/b]
One hit to the head = instant death. Any bullet that strikes the head or face will immediately trigger cardiac arrest through ACE Medical.

[b]🫀 Cumulative Chest Damage[/b]
Three hits to the chest or torso = death. The mod silently tracks damage to your torso - after the third hit, you're down. No second chances.

[b]💥 Realistic Explosion Lethality[/b]
Explosions now have distance-based lethality that scales with their size:
[list]
[*]Small explosions (grenades): ~5m lethal radius
[*]Medium explosions (RPG, small rockets): ~10m lethal radius
[*]Large explosions (bombs, artillery): ~20m lethal radius
[/list]

[h2]Why This Mod?[/h2]

ACE Medical is fantastic, but sometimes you want combat to be more lethal and decisive. This mod enhances ACE's damage system without replacing it, making firefights faster and more intense.

[b]Silent Operation[/b] - No notifications, no UI clutter. The mod works invisibly in the background for maximum immersion.

[b]Full ACE Integration[/b] - Uses ACE Medical's cardiac arrest system, compatible with all ACE medical settings and features.

[h2]Installation[/h2]

1. Subscribe to this mod
2. Subscribe to ACE and CBA_A3 if you haven't already
3. Launch Arma 3 and enable all three mods
4. Make sure load order is: CBA → ACE → ACE Damage Modifier

[h2]How It Works[/h2]

The mod uses event handlers to track damage to specific body parts. When lethal thresholds are reached, it triggers ACE Medical's cardiac arrest state. All tracking is done per-player and resets on respawn.

[b]Technical Details:[/b]
[list]
[*]Uses HandleDamage event for bullet/projectile tracking
[*]Uses Explosion event for blast damage
[*]Hit counters stored in player variables
[*]Fully compatible with ACE Advanced Medical
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
