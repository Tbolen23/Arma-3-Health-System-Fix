[h1]ACE Damage Modifier[/h1]

A balanced combat enhancement mod that works with ACE Medical to create more lethal AI combat while keeping players viable with medic support.

[h2]Key Features[/h2]

[b]🤖 AI Combat System[/b]
One hit to the head = instant death for AI. Three hits to chest or limbs = death. AI combat is fast and lethal.

[b]👤 Player Combat System[/b]
Two hits to the head = death for players (more forgiving). Six hits to chest or legs = death (allows for intense firefights).

[b]💊 Healing Resets Counters[/b]
THIS IS THE GAME CHANGER: When you receive ACE Medical treatment, your damage counters reset! Medics are now crucial for survival. Team gameplay is rewarded.

[b]💥 Realistic Explosion Lethality[/b]
Explosions affect everyone equally with distance-based lethality:
[list]
[*]Small explosions (grenades): ~5m lethal radius
[*]Medium explosions (RPG, small rockets): ~10m lethal radius
[*]Large explosions (bombs, artillery): ~20m lethal radius
[/list]

[h2]Why This Mod?[/h2]

Most damage mods treat players and AI the same, leading to either:
1. AI that takes too long to kill (boring)
2. Players that die instantly (frustrating)

This mod gives you the best of both worlds:
[list]
[*]AI goes down quickly (3 hits vs 6 for players)
[*]Players have more survivability (6 hits vs 3 for AI)
[*]Medics actually matter (healing resets counters)
[*]Teamwork is rewarded (stay with your medic!)
[/list]

[b]Silent Operation[/b] - No notifications, no UI clutter. The mod works invisibly in the background for maximum immersion.

[b]Full ACE Integration[/b] - Uses ACE Medical's cardiac arrest system, compatible with all ACE medical settings and features.

2. Subscribe to ACE and CBA_A3 if you haven't already
3. Launch Arma 3 and enable all three mods
4. Make sure load order is: CBA → ACE → ACE Damage Modifier

[h2]How It Works[/h2]

The mod uses event handlers to track damage to specific body parts. Different thresholds apply based on whether the unit is a player or AI. When healing occurs, player counters reset.

[b]Technical Details:[/b]
[list]
[*]Uses HandleDamage event for bullet/projectile tracking
[*]Uses Explosion event for blast damage
[*]Listens to ACE Medical treatment events
[*]Hit counters stored per-unit
[*]Healing resets player counters only
[*]AI gets 1 head/3 body thresholds
[*]Players get 2 head/6 body thresholds
[/list]
